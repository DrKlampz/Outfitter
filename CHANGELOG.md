# Gearhawk

## v0.11.11

- The loot roll advice panel is easier to read: bigger item icons, the item name on one line and the advice on the next, a colored bar at the side that matches the advice, and more room around everything

## v0.11.10

- Weapons are now judged as whole loadouts instead of one weapon against one slot: one-hander and shield, two daggers, a staff on its own, a one-hander with a held off-hand, or a two-hander, whichever pair is best for you
- Class, spec and level decide what is allowed: dual wield only for specs that have learned it, no two-handers for shield roles (Protection warrior and paladin, shield healers), no held off-hands for tanks, and nothing you cannot wield
- What to get / bags: if two weapons are only an upgrade together (a sword and a shield that beat your two-hander), both are listed with "as a pair with ..." and the combined gain
- Weapons you could already wield from your bags count as yours when judging items you do not own yet, so the list does not suggest a weapon your bags already beat

## v0.11.9

- Windows can be closed in combat


## v0.11.8

- New Professions tab: lists everything you wear or carry that raises a profession skill or speeds it up (fishing hats and poles, mining, herbalism and skinning gear, and reusable items). Everyday consumables such as lures are left out unless they are rare or better. For gear in your bags it shows which slot it would take, what it replaces and the overall change to your stats, and clicking it wears it (out of combat)
- The window is a little wider to fit the fifth tab
- New loot roll helper: when a group loot roll opens, a small panel says whether the item is an upgrade for your spec and which button to press (Need for upgrades and empty slots, greed for the rest). It only advises and never rolls for you. Turn it off, or have it also say it in chat, in the settings (Alt-click the minimap button); its window look can be changed there too

## v0.11.7

- What to get: items with the same upgrade percentage (like several at +100%) now stay in one fixed order instead of swapping places each time the list refreshes

## v0.11.6

- Redrawn anarchy symbol with a hand-brushed, worn look
- What to get: the list no longer flickers or jumps in size while item data is still loading; rows are only added, never dropped and re-added
- Smoother, cleaner drawings of several background symbols, plus a calligraphic version of every symbol
- Saved data is found again when the game reports an empty realm name

## v0.11.5

- New background symbols: sword, crossed swords, shield, sun and paw print
- The background symbol can now be rotated and mirrored

## v0.11.4

- New background symbols: Celtic cross, anarchy, peace sign, skull and crossbones, radiation, atom, infinity, lightning bolt, anchor, Helm of Awe, valknut, Eye of Providence, triskele and fleur-de-lis
- The background symbol can now be moved left, right, up and down, and resized
- Picking a color for a piece of artwork now turns its own-color option on, so the color changes at once

## v0.11.3
- Every piece of artwork (corners, crest, edge pattern, title banner, divider, middle emblem) can now have its own color. New "Your file" emblem slots show your own faction, race and city images from the Media folder.

## v0.11.2
- More emblems: religious, Wiccan, Pagan, Druid, Satanic and occult symbols from many faiths, and the names are tidied.
- The artwork can have a color of its own (Artwork section): corners, crest, edges, banner, divider and the middle emblem.
- The close button in the top right can be restyled (the game's X, boxed, plain, red, filled or hidden) with its own size and color.
- The game-icon emblems (faction crests, race and city icons) are removed; looks that used them show your class emblem.
- Fixed the list preview staying open after a pick; the divider under the title no longer runs through the search row.

## v0.11.1

- Pointing at an entry in the emblem, corner, crest, edge, banner and divider lists shows a small preview of it.

## v0.11.0
- What to get: fixed the list flickering and the window jumping. Items the game never sends data for are now skipped after a few tries instead of re-checking forever.
- Gradients: pick both colors, each end's opacity, vertical, horizontal, diagonal, middle-out or center-to-borders, over or instead of the background texture; plus a theme with its own emblem, corners, crest and edge for every talent spec, and a "My spec" automatic theme.
- Look profiles: save your look under a name and switch between saved looks at any time; every character remembers which profile it wears (Look panel, top).
- World of Warcraft flavored art, all optional: nine class themes (Warrior to Druid) with an original emblem, corner pieces, crest and colors for each class, plus a "My class (automatic art)" theme that picks yours; Blue and gold kingdom, Red and iron war camp, Moonlit grove and Plaguelands themes; new spiked corners, horn, crescent and skull crests, and an iron spike edge. The emblem in the middle of the window can also show your class or your talent tree using the game's own icons. Find them in the window options under Colors > Theme and Artwork.
- More optional artwork for the window box: ornament strips along the edges (rope, chain, pearls, runes, vine), a banner behind the title (ribbon, riveted plaque, scroll, jeweled plate), a divider with an ornament under the title, and a faint emblem in the middle of the window (compass rose, shield, laurel wreath, star, runic rings). All in the new Artwork section of the window options; the eight art themes now use them, and the crest sits above the top edge.
- New optional artwork in the window look: seven textured backgrounds (wood planks, aged paper, arcane starfield, runed slate, riveted iron, damask tapestry, dragon scales), five carved corner pieces (filigree, vines, runes, iron plates, arcane sigil), four crests for the top edge (winged, jeweled, scrollwork, crown), and eight ready-made art themes (Arcane library, Dwarven forge, Elven glade, Old tome, Runestone, Royal court, Dragonscale, Oak lodge). Pick them in the window options (Colors > Theme, Background, Border > Corner artwork and Crest). Everything is off until you choose it and syncs between your add-ons like the rest of the look.
- Shares one Auction House scan with Profiteer when both are installed: Gearhawk never asks the game for a scan itself, skips reading the list when Profiteer already saved it, and uses Profiteer's prices when they are newer than its own.
- Works completely on its own. Extras that use another installed add-on's saved data (auction prices, gear your other characters carry or can craft) are used only when that add-on is loaded, and the Auction tab reads Gearhawk's own scan first. The wording no longer names other add-ons.
- Removed the add-on-to-add-on theming hooks and cross-add-on names from the window look code; the shared look sync between your add-ons works the same.
- The "Pulse the flair" option now also pulses the edge finish (soft, bevel, double line and glow), not just the corner studs.

## v0.10.6
- Look sync: the first time your add-ons meet, they now all take on the same look right away instead of waiting for you to change something.

## v0.10.5
- Your custom look is now shared with your other add-ons: change colors, background, border or opacity in one and the rest follow (the newest change wins). It is on by default; untick "Sync my look with my other add-ons" in the Look window to turn it off.

## v0.10.4
- Fixed "script ran too long" errors that could happen in combat. The "What to get" search now works in short slices, waits until combat ends, and the bag arrows and alerts are held until the fight is over.

## v0.10.3
- "What to get" no longer lists dungeons your faction can't get into: Hall of Thanes and the Stockade are hidden for Horde characters, and Ragefire Chasm and Ruins of Lordaeron are hidden for Alliance characters.

## v0.10.2
- Stat weights revisited for every class and spec: Warlocks now value Stamina and Spirit properly (Affliction and Demonology especially), and Intellect is no longer over-weighted for casters, healers and Balance druids. Feral cat druids value Strength above Agility, and Retribution paladins value a little more Intellect.

## v0.10.1
- Upgrade percentages are measured against a typical item you wear, so a trivial difference such as 3 armor against 7 armor on a cloak no longer shows as +133%.

## v0.10.0
- Renamed from Outfitter to Gearhawk, because another add-on already uses the name Outfitter and the two would clash. Commands are now `/hawk` and `/gearhawk`. Settings start fresh under the new name.

## v0.9.0
- Bags: the green upgrade arrow now shows in third-party bag windows, and upgrades also get a green frame and a faint green wash over the icon, so they stand out without hovering.
- Fonts: the font pack now ships inside this add-on (Fonts folder), so the separate Look Fonts add-on is no longer needed, and there are about 60 more fonts to pick from under Look > Font.

## v0.8.9
- What to get: the Quest, Dungeon, Battleground and Vendor boxes no longer cover the last item; the window is taller to make room for them.

## v0.8.8
- Quest rewards: cleaner marks. The best upgrade gets a green outline around its icon, and every real upgrade gets a small strip along the bottom of its icon with the percentage (or "new" for an empty slot). Nothing floats over the text above the buttons any more.

## v0.8.7
- New: green arrow on the best upgrades in your bags (Look settings); two-part character name fix; /issues log.

## v0.8.6
- What to get: tick boxes along the bottom (Quest, Dungeon, Battleground, Vendor) choose which kinds of gear each character sees. Saved per character; everything is on until you turn it off. Honor and mark vendors and the battleground bosses count as Battleground; crafted gear is always shown.
- What to get: Outfitter now remembers what each vendor really has for sale when you open their window. If you have visited every vendor listed for an item and none of them sells it (like Kelm Hargunth's empty window in The Barrens), the item drops to the bottom of the list with a red "You checked <vendor>: not for sale right now" line instead of a cost.

## v0.8.5
- Tooltip: a huge upgrade percentage (over 300%, like +1300% on a low-level cloak) now reads "big upgrade", the same as the window does.
- Checked every class and spec against every armor type, shield, weapon type, two-hander and relic: Libram, Idol and Totem now only count for Paladins, Druids and Shamans, and two-handed swords, axes and maces are flagged for the classes that can't use them (Rogues, Mages, Warlocks, Priests and so on).
- The upgrade alert for another character also says "a big upgrade" instead of a huge percentage.
- "Allows X% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting" is now counted (healers value it most), along with "Increases your spell damage by up to N" wording.

## v0.8.4
- Healers on shield classes use a one-hander and shield; tanks are never offered a two-hander; gold trim window flair; alert for upgrades held by other characters; tooltip no longer guesses on grouped random-suffix auction entries.
- Warriors: a bow, gun or thrown weapon no longer scores like a main hand weapon (it is only for pulling), which was making ranged weapons look like huge upgrades. Protection weights rebalanced so armor, dodge, parry and block are worth what they are next to stamina.
- Characters window (shift-click the minimap button): delete a character's saved spec, and deleted characters are spotted and removed automatically.

## v0.8.3
- Tooltips now also show in Dialogue UI windows.
- Protection Paladin: shields, block and defense weigh properly, and the spec is detected by talent tree name, so Hammer vs Talonstrike style comparisons read right.
- Class audit: Hunters value melee weapons by their stats, and dual-wield classes get off-hand suggestions.
- Auction tab: when nothing shows, it now says why (too high a level, unusable, no better, not gear).

## v0.8.0
- Tabs renamed: Bag upgrades is now Inventory, What I wear is now Equipped, Where to get is now What to get.
- New: window customization. Alt-click the minimap button to restyle each window separately (or all together): background textures, colors with copy/paste, opacity sliders, border styles, fonts, scale and position lock. Over 50 fonts to pick from (install the LookFonts folder for the bundled ones; SharedMedia fonts show up too). Off until you tick "Use a custom look"; unticking restores the original. All of the add-on's options now live in that same window, on an Options tab, and the window itself wears the first window's look.

## v0.7.0
- Tooltips: hovering any item now adds an Outfitter line (green UPGRADE with the % over what you wear, red worse, grey same, or can't use). It uses the current client's tooltip hook, so it now actually appears; `/outfit tip` toggles it.
- Wands now score by their damage per second for casters (before, a wand was valued only for its stats, so wand upgrades never showed up).
- About 750 more boss drops added from AzerothCompendium (v0.7.10) loot tables, covering Forever-added gear in the classic dungeons and bosses that were missing (e.g. Azshir the Sleepless).
- Proofread the source data against AzerothCompendium's loot tables: removed Scourge Invasion event mobs (Balzaphon, Revanchion, Lord Blackwood, Sever, Lady Falther'ess) that were listed as dungeon bosses; fixed Staff of Dominance (Golemagg, Molten Core) and Rod of the Sleepwalker (Twilight Lord Kelris, Blackfathom Deeps); dropped an entry with no known source.
- Removed Scorn's Icy Choker's bogus source (Scorn is a Scourge Invasion event mob, not in Scarlet Monastery).
- Scoring fix: Warlocks were being offered healing gear. Pure healing items (healing power, no spell damage) now get half credit for specs that don't heal, and Warlock weights for Spirit, mana regen and Stamina were lowered (Life Tap makes them worth far less than spell damage).
- Dungeon and raid drops are now accurate: every item lists the actual bosses that drop it and the instance (up to three bosses, bosses only; ordinary monsters only when nothing else drops it). Before, the first monster in the quest database was shown, often a trash mob or the wrong place.
- WoW Forever's own dungeons are covered: The Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands and City of Dalaran, with their bosses and gear. Classic's database did not know these dungeons, so their drops were missing or unplaced.
- Gear that WoW Forever added to the original dungeons (for example new drops in Blackfathom Deeps, Gnomeregan, Maraudon and Dire Maul) is included, and named bosses such as Quartermaster Zigris are now recognised as bosses.
- Sources: the Classic loot database (CMaNGOS) for the original dungeons; WoW Forever loot tables compiled by the community (wowtbc.gg, AzerothCompendium) for the new ones. Loot in the newest Forever dungeons is still being discovered.

## v0.6.2
- Weapons whose skill you still have to learn from a trainer (for example one-handed swords for a Warlock) are no longer suggested; they show as "train Swords" in /outfit weapons.
- New `/outfit weapons` lists the weapons in your level range with their score against what you hold.
- Where to get rows are roomier so wrapped text and costs no longer crowd the next item.
- Where to get shows every result instead of cutting off with "+N more".
- Long source lines (several vendors, long zone names) now wrap inside the window instead of running off the edge.
- Vendor items show what they cost: gold, honor, arena points or trade items. Prices are remembered once you open that vendor (honor, tokens and reputation-discounted prices); until then it falls back to a bundled list of vendor gold prices (Classic database), and says unknown only if the item isn't on it.

## v0.6.1
- Quest rewards are back for every class: a bundled list of Classic quest rewards (armor and weapons, with each quest's level, race and class limits and zone) is combined with the Questie vendor and dungeon data. Quest rewards show as "Quest: name - zone".
- Items that would give you nothing (fishing hats and buckets, anything with no stats you use) are no longer suggested for empty slots.
- Off-hand pieces are not suggested while you are using a two-handed weapon.
- Where to get shows the real colored item names once the game knows them.

## v0.6.0
- Where to get now works for every class with real quest, vendor and dungeon sources. It reads the quest database that Questie installs (QuestieDB) and lists, for your class, race and level, armor and weapons from quest rewards ("Quest: name - zone"), vendors ("Vendor: name, zone") and dungeon bosses ("Drop: boss, dungeon"), each checked against what you wear. The built-in Mage/Warlock/Priest lists still add their own notes.
- The database is read once per session in small slices a few seconds after login, so there is no hitch; opening Where to get before it finishes shows "Loading..." and fills in by itself.
- Needs Questie (QuestieDB) installed; without it you still get the built-in lists and your other characters' gear and recipes.

## v0.5.7
- Where to get no longer offers gear you cannot actually get: bind-on-pickup crafts only show if your own character has the profession ("Leatherworking - you know the recipe"), and soulbound or bind-on-pickup gear sitting on another character is no longer offered (it cannot be passed on). Bind-on-equip crafts by your other characters still show.
- Auction tab rows now show the real colored item names instead of raw "item:1234:::..." text.
- Weapons your class cannot use at all (a Druid with a sword, a Mage with an axe, and so on) are no longer suggested.

## v0.5.6
- Read the "Increases healing done by up to X and damage done by up to Y for all magical spells and effects" line (it was ignored, so pieces carrying it were undervalued and swaps looked much bigger than they are, e.g. +114% instead of about +21%).
- More stat line forms understood: "+N Spell Damage/Power", "+N Healing Spells", "+N <school> Damage", "+N mana every 5 sec", "+N Attack Power", ranged attack power, and armor with a bonus. Bonuses that only apply "when fighting" something are no longer counted.
- Enchants and gems are not counted twice (the game already puts them in the stat lines).
- Swapping out a piece of an active set now counts the set bonus you would lose.
- New `/outfit unread` lists any stat lines on your worn gear that Outfitter does not understand.

## v0.5.5
- Single-school spell damage ("+10 Frost damage") is now only worth what your spec actually casts from that school: Frost pants are no longer an upgrade for an Affliction Warlock, but are for a Frost Mage. Each spec has its own school weights (Affliction: Shadow, some Fire; Destruction: Fire, some Shadow; and so on).
- "Restores N Mana per 5 sec" is now read (the game capitalizes Mana), so mana regeneration is counted. Before this, a gear piece with MP5 was under-valued and weak swaps looked like upgrades.

## v0.5.4
- Where to get now names the Scarlet Monastery wing (Graveyard, Library, Cathedral) and the Dire Maul wing for the drops that come from there, e.g. "Scarlet Monastery Graveyard - Bloodmage Thalnos".

## v0.5.3
- Gear you cannot use yet no longer gets suggested: level, profession rank ("Requires Blacksmithing (125)"), class ("Classes: ..."), and armor type your class cannot wear yet (plate/mail before level 40, shields for non-shield classes) are now checked from the item text itself, not only from the tooltip's red color. The item's minimum level from the game is used as a backup.

## v0.5.2
- Fixed the actual cause of the Auction tab freezing: the game rejected a level lookup (UnitLevel needs "player"), so no item was ever checked.
- The Auction tab can no longer get stuck on "Checking auction items... 0 of N": every batch is error-protected, a stalled check restarts itself, and new auction data replaces a check that was running on old data.
- `/outfit debug` now prints the auction state (saved listings, progress, errors) so a problem can be reported precisely.

## v0.5.1
- Auction tab no longer sticks at "Checking auction items... 0 of N". It now reads the live Auction House listing itself (every item with its real random suffix, e.g. "of Frozen Wrath"), so stats are scored correctly, and one unreadable item can no longer stop the whole check.
- Shows up to 12 buys instead of 8, and says how many items could not be read.
- Open the Auction House and let the scan finish (Profiteer's scan is picked up automatically), then open the Auction tab.

## v0.5.0
- Where to get now works for every class: it lists gear your other characters hold in their bags or bank ("Alt: in Barry's bags") and gear your characters know how to craft ("Crafted: Blacksmithing - Barry knows the recipe"), each checked against your class, level and what you wear. This reads the data Profiteer saves, so Profiteer must be installed.
- The built-in quest/dungeon/vendor lists are still Mage, Warlock and Priest only.

- Empty slots now rank by how good the item is, not just by price.
- Tabs renamed to fit four: Bag upgrades, What I wear, Where to get, Auction.
